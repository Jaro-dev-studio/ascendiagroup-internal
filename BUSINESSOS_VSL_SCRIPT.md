# BusinessOS VSL — Structure & Script

Target length: **2:00**
Format: full-screen screen recording of the animated `/business-os-lp` page, presenter in picture-in-picture
Tone: founder to founder, casual
Audience: agencies and service businesses
Goal: book the free consultation

---

## The one structural decision

Scrolling the landing page alone will not carry the proof beat. The page describes what we do; it does not show software. In a 2-minute VSL for custom software, the highest-leverage 20 seconds is real product footage.

So the script cuts away from the landing page in the middle, shows `studio.jaro.dev` and the Hawaii Life system, then returns to the page for the mechanism and CTA. Everything else stays on the animated LP.

---

## Structure

Classic VSL beats compressed to 2 minutes, mapped onto the page's actual scroll order.

| Beat | Time | On screen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hook | 0:00–0:11 | Hero |
| Agitate | 0:11–0:31 | Problem section + pain points grid |
| Reframe | 0:31–0:44 | Solution header |
| Proof | 0:44–1:08 | Cut away to real software |
| Mechanism + speed | 1:08–1:23 | 6 phases |
| Risk reversal | 1:23–1:37 | ROI calculator |
| CTA | 1:37–1:50 | CTA button |

The hook has no intro, no name, and no company background. The reframe gets its own beat because ownership is the actual differentiator, not speed or AI.

## Script

Roughly 285 words, which lands at about 1:55 at a natural pace with pauses.

### Hook — 0:00

**Screen:** Hero, fresh page load so the typewriter types "BusinessOS".

> If you run an agency or a service business, you're renting five different tools, using about twenty percent of each one, and still doing half the work by hand. Let me show you the alternative.

### Agitate — 0:11

**Screen:** Scroll to "Your Business is Growing / But Your Operations Are Breaking", then let the pain points grid animate in. Linger here.

> This is what growth actually looks like from the inside. Workflows that only John understands. Tools duct-taped together with Zapier and spreadsheets. Someone leaves, and something breaks. You're rebuilding the same report in Excel every single week. None of this shows up on your P&L. It just quietly eats your margin.

### Reframe — 0:31

**Screen:** Scroll to "The Solution / How We Transform Your Operations". Pause on the header.

> So we build you one system instead. Custom software, shaped around how your business actually runs, not how some SaaS company decided it should. And you own it. Outright, forever. No seats, no subscription.

### Proof — 0:44

**Screen:** Cut away. Full screen `studio.jaro.dev`, click into the Meta Ads section. Then cut to the Hawaii Life system.

> And this isn't theory. This is our own BusinessOS, it's what runs Jaro. Meta Ads spend pulled live from the API, so performance is real-time, not a Monday morning export. And this is Hawaii Life: custom lead routing, AI lead scoring, spam filtering, an automated SEO content engine, and a CRM built specifically for real estate. Whatever your process is, we can build it.

### Mechanism + speed — 1:08

**Screen:** Back to the LP. Scroll the six phases briskly, let a couple animate in.

> We do it in six phases. Unify your data, automate the reporting, standardize the messy processes, then layer AI on top. And because we build AI-heavy, you'll see your first version live in three days. The full thing in fourteen.

### Risk reversal — 1:23

**Screen:** ROI calculator. Drag a slider so the number moves on camera.

> Drop your numbers in here and you'll see roughly what this is costing you right now. On the call we do it properly. We map the entire system, calculate your savings, and you get a fixed price and a timeline.

### CTA — 1:37

**Screen:** Scroll so the CTA is centered, move the cursor onto the button and hover it. Hold the last frame.

> No pitch, no obligation, you leave with the plan either way. Book the free consultation and let's see what your BusinessOS looks like.

## Alternate hooks

Swap any of these into the 0:00–0:11 slot. Each is written to run 10 to 13 seconds and hand off cleanly into the Agitate beat. Record two or three and test.

### 1. Own vs rent (baseline, in the script above)

> If you run an agency or a service business, you're renting five different tools, using about twenty percent of each one, and still doing half the work by hand. Let me show you the alternative.

Broadest appeal. Leads with waste, which almost everyone recognises immediately. Use this as the control.

### 2. Key-person risk

> Every service business has a John. He's the only one who knows how half of it actually works. And if John leaves tomorrow, you're in real trouble. Let me show you how we fix that permanently.

Hits hardest for owners of 10 to 50 person teams. More emotional than the cost angle, and it flows straight into the pain points grid because "only John knows" is literally the first card.

### 3. Pattern interrupt

> Count the tabs your team has open right now. That's not a tech stack, that's a tax you pay every single day. Here's what we build instead.

Shortest and punchiest. Best for paid traffic where you need to win the first three seconds. Weaker for warm or referred traffic, where it can feel gimmicky.

### 4. Speed

> Three days from now, you could be looking at a working piece of custom software built specifically for how your business runs. Not a demo, not a mockup, live. Let me show you how.

Leads with the most surprising claim we have. Highest stopping power, but it sets a promise the rest of the video has to pay off, so keep the 3-day and 14-day numbers prominent later.

### 5. Contrarian

> Stop buying software. If your business is doing real numbers, you should be building it and owning it. Let me show you what that actually looks like.

Strong for LinkedIn and founder audiences who like a take. Polarising by design, so it will convert a smaller slice harder.

### 6. Cost of the status quo

> Most agencies we talk to are losing somewhere between ten and twenty hours a week to work that shouldn't involve a human at all. And they're paying for the tools that create it. Here's the fix.

Most logical of the six and the best fit if the traffic is colder or more skeptical. Pairs naturally with the ROI calculator later in the video.

## Recording notes

Specific to this page:

- **Hard reload before each take** so the typewriter animation and the hero stagger actually play.
- **Scroll slower than feels natural.** The section reveals are scroll-triggered, so if you outrun them they pop in half-finished.
- **Do not click "Watch 2-Min Demo"** during the take. That opens this video inside itself.
- **Put the PiP bottom-right.** The floating "Book Free Consultation" button is pinned top-right once you pass the hero, and you want it visible for the whole back half.
- **1920x1080, browser zoom at about 90%** so more of each section fits in frame. Clean browser, no bookmarks bar, no extra tabs.
- **Smooth trackpad scroll, not a scroll wheel.** Jerky scrolling reads as amateur more than anything else in the video.
- **Three takes, don't watch them back in between**, then pick.

The script is a structure, not a teleprompter. Let the wording drift where it feels stiff. The only lines worth keeping close to verbatim are "you own it, outright, forever, no seats, no subscription" and the three days / fourteen days numbers.
